How it works, step by step

  1. Tell Gigwren what you sell

    Choose your profession or add your own keywords, like "need a logo designer".

  2. Pick the groups to watch

    Use the group search to find communities on Facebook, join them, and add them to your list.

  3. Gigwren checks for new requests

    New posts are compared with your keywords, and on Pro with the meaning of what you do.

  4. You get the lead and reply

    Open the post, send a clear message with a price, and mark the lead as contacted.

Questions people ask

Can I see it before I pay?

Yes. Watch the live demo on the home page. Plus also starts with a 7 day free trial.

Which communities does it watch?

The groups you choose. Today the dashboard shows sample posts so you can see how matching works. Live monitoring of real groups is the next part we are building.

Does Gigwren message people for me?

On Plus and Pro, no. You decide who to reply to and write the message yourself. On DFY, our team also sends outreach messages for you.

What does AI matching do?

It reads the meaning of a post, so it can flag a request like "my site looks old and nobody visits it" even if it never says "web designer".

Is my information safe?

During early access we ask only for your name, email and profession, and never a password. Your setup is stored on your own device.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel any time.
